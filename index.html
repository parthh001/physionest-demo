FAQ

Good to know

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. You can book directly. If you have reports, X-rays or MRI scans, bring them to your first visit so we can understand your history.

What happens in the first session?

We start with a detailed assessment of your pain, posture and movement, explain what we find, and agree on a treatment plan together.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition and how long you've had it. We'll give you a clear estimate after your assessment.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, loose clothing that lets you move freely and gives easy access to the area being treated.

Are you open on Sundays?

We're open Monday to Saturday, 8:30 AM to 9 PM. Early and late slots work well around office hours.
